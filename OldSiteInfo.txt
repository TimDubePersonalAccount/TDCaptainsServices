Launch Service
Hudson TowBoat operates a 24/7 launch service throughout our service region.



Hudson TowBoat is ready to help you transport your people and goods efficiently. We are based out of Alpine Marina but

our launch can operate out of almost any marina on the Hudson River.



Our launch service is great for:

​

Harbor pilots

Crew changes on tugs and barges

Dispatch marine techs to tugs and barges

Moving food and supplies to anchored vessels

Delivering marine parts to vessels across the river

Emergency medical personnel transport

Plus many other uses and scenarios
